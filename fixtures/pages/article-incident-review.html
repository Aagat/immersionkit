Queue Drift After a Quiet Deploy

A short incident review about a delayed worker rollout, alert timing, and the notes that made the recovery easier to audit.

Timeline

During the first hour, the on-call lead can light up the timeline with obvious events, but this does not explain the queue. The useful detail was hidden right after the deploy marker, where a small retry window doubled.

Engineers watched the worker count move up slowly while dashboards stayed green. That looked fine until support reported that export jobs were finishing in the wrong order.

What Changed

The team decided to plant a manual check beside the automated alert until the next release. This is intentionally boring: a human can watch the first queue drain before the rollout moves on. 

rollback:
  owner: platform
  manual_check: first-queue-drain
  alert_window: 15m

Follow-up

The permanent fix is to make the retry counter stable across workers. If the counter jumps, the deploy should pause before the user-facing queue is touched.
