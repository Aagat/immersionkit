Reading a Small Metrics Notebook

A walkthrough for checking whether a weekly metrics notebook is telling a stable story or merely producing attractive charts.

Start With the Shape

Like a bug report, the notebook needs a boring title and a clear owner. This means zero rows can move through the pipeline without a timestamp, even when the model is still experimental.

The current model is fine for a first review, but it should not decide whether the launch is safe. We left the control chart open because the next cohort has a different traffic pattern.

Keep the Calculation Visible

The most useful notebook cell explains why a small period of missing data changes the rate. Without that note, a reviewer might mistake sampling noise for product movement. 

weekly_rate =
  completed_events / eligible_sessions
guardrail = missing_timestamps == 0

Review Notes

A notebook can be precise without being long. The goal is to help another person reproduce the case and spot the first assumption that might be wrong.
