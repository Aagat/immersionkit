Regional Rail Service Adds New Coastal Stops

Transport Reporter: Noel Harper

A new timetable gives commuters faster options between the harbor district, the university quarter, and the northern suburbs.

What Changed This Week

Officials added early departures, reduced long transfer waits, and posted clearer signs in stations that were often confusing for visitors.

Riders said the updated routes feel more predictable because trains arrive steadily and announcements are easier to follow.

Context

The rail authority tested the same plan during winter storms and reported fewer late arrivals than expected.
