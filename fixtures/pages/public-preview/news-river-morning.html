Daily River Ledger

Morning crews reopen the low river trail after a night of rain

The public works team says the city can watch the water line without closing the park, but volunteers still plan to help at least one family each morning.

Transport Weather Schools

As soon as the river gauge fell below the warning line, the morning crew opened the small bridge near the old city garden. The path is narrow right now, so workers placed signs at the school gate and near the water station.

The update matters because commuters used to cut through the park before taking the early train. City staff said the route can stay open if the light rain remains steady and the right bank does not move overnight.

Several neighbors plan to watch the school crossing while the bus stop is moved up the street. A volunteer coordinator said no one should plant a temporary sign in the bike lane, because the corner is already busy before work.

The city will publish another update in the morning if the forecast changes. For now, the trail is open, the picnic field is closed, and the public restroom near the garden remains under repair.

The office of public safety published a weather forecast update and a school board meeting schedule before the train station entrance opened for the first group of visitors.

At a glance
